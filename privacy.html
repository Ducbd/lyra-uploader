Privacy Policy — Lyra Audiobook Publisher

Effective date: 9 October 2026

  • Who uses it: only the owner of the YouTube channel Lyra Tinh Vân, on their own computer. The app is not offered to other users.
  • Google data accessed: the single OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used only to upload the owner's own video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, always as private videos.
  • What is stored: the OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. The app keeps a local log of uploaded video IDs. No Google user data is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI model training.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the local token file also stops the app.
  • Contact: via the YouTube channel Lyra Tinh Vân.
